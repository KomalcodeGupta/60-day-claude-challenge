rapid hiring phase 

A rapid hiring phase is a period when a company speeds up its recruitment process to fill multiple vacancies in a short amount of time. This often happens because of business growth, new projects, seasonal demand, or urgent staffing needs. The goal is to reduce the time between application and onboarding while still selecting qualified candidates.

Characteristics of a Rapid Hiring Phase
Faster resume screening
Shorter interview process (often 1–2 rounds)
Quick hiring decisions
Large number of open positions
Faster offer letters and onboarding
Use of virtual interviews and online assessments
Why Companies Use Rapid Hiring
Business expansion
New client projects
Seasonal demand
Replacing employees quickly
Launching new products or services
Example

A software company wins a large client project and needs 100 Software Developers within one month. Instead of the usual four interview rounds, it conducts:

Online coding test
One technical interview
HR discussion
Offer letter within a few days

This is an example of a rapid hiring phase.

Advantages
Faster recruitment
Reduced vacancies
Quicker project execution
Better candidate experience through shorter wait times
Challenges
Less time to evaluate candidates
Risk of poor hiring decisions if screening is rushed
Increased workload for recruiters and hiring managers
